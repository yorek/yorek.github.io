Demoscene experiments

These are experiments where I use AI to rebuild the kind of demoscene effects I used to enjoy back in the early days of coding on my 486-compatible computer. It is a way for me to get confident with AI, have fun, and revisit the visual ideas that made the demoscene so fascinating. It is also a way for me to explore how much detail I need to provide to an AI when I am trying to build something complex like the effect visible here: how much hand-holding is required, how good my instructions need to be, and how much clarity and structure leads to better results.

The goal is not to reproduce a specific old demo exactly, but to explore the techniques, the aesthetic, and the feeling of those early graphics experiments in a modern browser.
